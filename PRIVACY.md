# Privacy policy: Feed Quiet for Facebook

Feed Quiet operates locally in your Chrome browser. It examines elements on supported Facebook pages to hide recognized Reels and Stories according to your settings.

The extension stores filter preferences, pause state, and the words or phrases you enter for keyword filtering using Chrome's local extension storage. These settings remain on your device. It does not retain or transmit feed content, browsing history, account information, or usage analytics, and does not sell or share your settings. It has no backend, telemetry, advertising, or external network requests. Restore defaults clears saved keywords.

Facebook page access is used to identify and hide matching content. The storage permission is used to remember your preferences. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Facebook's own data handling is separate from this extension.
