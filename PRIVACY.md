# Privacy policy: Feed Quiet for Facebook

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Feed Quiet for Facebook is developed by Tal Ayash. It operates locally in your Chrome browser to hide content according to your selected filters, including Reels, Stories, sponsored posts, suggestions, videos, keyword matches, sidebar shortcuts, and the home feed.

## Information processed on your device

The extension examines Facebook page content, including text, links, and page elements, and checks the current Facebook URL to determine which filters apply. This processing takes place in browser memory. Page content and URLs are not retained in extension storage or transmitted to the developer or third parties. The extension does not maintain a browsing-history log.

## Settings and retention

The extension stores filter preferences, pause state, and the words or phrases you enter for keyword filtering using Chrome's local extension storage. These settings remain on your device. It does not retain or transmit feed content, browsing history, account information, or usage analytics, and does not sell or share your settings. It has no backend, telemetry, advertising, or external network requests. Restore defaults clears saved keywords.

Facebook page access is used to identify and hide matching content. The storage permission is used to remember your preferences. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Facebook's own data handling is separate from this extension.

Preferences and keywords remain in local extension storage until you change them, restore defaults, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall the extension. You can edit or clear keywords in the popup. Pausing filtering preserves your settings for later use.

## Sharing and limited use

Feed Quiet does not sell, transfer, or share page content or settings with third parties. Information is used only to provide the filtering features you select, not for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. The developer has no remote access to your page content or saved settings.

Feed Quiet's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through [the project's GitHub issues](https://github.com/talayash/facebook-feed-control/issues). Please do not include private Facebook content, sensitive keywords, or account credentials in public issues.
